# Short videos (TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels)

Faceless, real, slow enough to read. One render goes to all three platforms.

## Format

- 1080x1920, 15 to 35 s. Pace like a person using the app, not a trailer: about 28 s for a full idea (owner's
  preference from the app-preview work: real flow, slow, no template filler).
- First second: a plain text hook that states the useful thing ("What this sentence really says", the word, the
  question). No "Wait for it", no countdowns.
- Body: the real app capture (screen recording or a `promo-assets` scene built from real captures), with large
  captions so it works muted. Keep the top 15% and bottom 25% free of key text (platform UI covers them).
- End: the app name and where to get it (live stores only), 2 s. No "link in bio!!" voice; the caption carries it.
- Audio: the app's own sounds, or quiet music from the platform's own licensed library added at posting time.
  The owner's voice only if a clip truly needs it. AI voice from the app is labelled "AI voice" on screen or muted.
- Credits on screen for any third-party content, in the form its license asks for (e.g. "Text: Wikipedia,
  CC BY-SA 4.0, adapted").

## Making one

1. Pick a series and an item from the product brief's "safe to show" list.
2. Capture it in the real app (the product session knows how: device, simulator, or web build).
3. Check by hand: every word, gloss, vowel mark, number and answer on screen. Write what you checked in the packet
   ("checked: meanings of all 6 tapped words, verb table row 3").
4. Build it with `promo-assets` (vertical short template for the brand); run `stills.py` and look at the stills.
5. Write the caption (voice.md), 2 to 4 hashtags, and the credit line.
6. Hand it over in the week's packet with the file path. The owner posts.

## Series that fit the owner's constraints

- "What this sentence really says": tap through one sentence word by word.
- "One X, every form": one item, the app's full breakdown.
- "Same thing, two/three variants": side-by-side comparisons the app does well.
- "Before / after": a real problem, then the app solving it (Checkpoint: the patch changed this answer).
- Build-in-public clips (Side Quest Nexus account): a real bug and its fix, a real number.

Each product's brief lists its own series and their limits.
