# Channels

What each place expects, and what to check before the first post there.

## Reddit

- Read the subreddit's rules and sidebar before anything; record the self-promotion rule in the product brief.
  Most allow little or none outside a weekly thread, and many apply the "9:1" norm (nine helpful contributions per
  one about your own thing).
- Start with helpful replies that don't mention the product. Mention it only where it directly answers the
  question, with "I made this".
- One account per person (the owner's), not per product; product accounts on Reddit read as spam.
- Never ask friends to upvote; never post the same thing to many subreddits at once.

## Discord

- Join, read the rules and the channel list, find the promotion or showcase channel if there is one.
- Be a member first. DMs to strangers about the product: never.

## TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels

- One account per product per platform (audiences don't overlap across products).
- Same vertical file to all three; captions differ slightly (TikTok and Reels: 2 to 4 hashtags; Shorts: title
  plus a short description with the link).
- Consistency beats volume: 2 a week, every week, for a pilot.
- Reply to comments in the owner's voice (drafted by Claude, sent by the owner).
- Business vs creator account: start as a creator/personal account (more music options); switch later if ads or
  analytics need it.

## Bluesky and X

- One Side Quest Nexus account for build-in-public posts across products, not one per app.
- Real stories from the work: a bug hunt, a number, a decision. Link the product only when the story is about it.

## Email outreach (universities, creators, wikis, newsletters)

- One person at a time, named, about something specific to them ("your second-year Arabic course", "your wiki's
  patch notes page"). No mail merge.
- Short: who you are (one line), what you made (one line), why it might help them specifically, one easy ask.
- Offer only what exists (e.g. arabayya's free tier; no promo codes until they're set up).
- Send from a Side Quest Nexus or product address, not personal. One follow-up after a week at most.
- Log every send and reply in `log.tsv`.

## Before a new place

Add to the product brief: the place, its rules (self-promotion, links, flair), who's there, and what kind of post
works there. Then a 2-week stretch of helpful participation before any product mention.
