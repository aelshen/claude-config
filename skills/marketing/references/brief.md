# Product brief template (`marketing/BRIEF.md` in each product repo)

The product's own session owns this file and keeps it true. Marketing work reads it first.

```markdown
# <Product>: marketing brief

Updated <date> by <session>.

## One line
<What it is, for whom, in plain words. No slogans.>

## Who it's for
<The real audience, in their words. Who it is not for.>

## Positioning rules
<Musts and must-nots, e.g. "for learners of any dialect; never lead with Egyptian".>

## Live where
<Stores and web, exact. What is pending (never mention as available).>

## Safe to show
| Area | State | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| <feature> | solid / early (say so) / not ready (never show) | <limits> |

## Content and licensing
<Which sources may appear on screen, with the exact credit line. Which may not.>

## Claims
<Allowed claims with proof; claims never to make (and why, e.g. what reviewers were told). Exact prices.>

## Series
<Short-video series that fit, with what each may show.>

## Places
| Place | Rules (self-promo, links) | Audience | What works there |
|---|---|---|---|

## Assets
<Brand folder, captures, existing videos, store listing text.>

## Cost notes
<What makes a clip cost money (model calls, voice) and roughly how much.>
```
